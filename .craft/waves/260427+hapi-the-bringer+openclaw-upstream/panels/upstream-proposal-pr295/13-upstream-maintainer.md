## Strengths

- **The "every policy decision anchored to LKG-fingerprinted state at a known time" framing is genuinely good.** It's a design statement that reduces to a contract change, not a feature. That's the shape upstream wants from enterprise contributors. The earlier `realigned-story.md` v2 chain had the same shape with snap; this version has it with LKG. Either substrate could carry it; the framing is solid.
- **The pre-RFC actually answers "why upstream"** in three concrete bullets and one of them is the right one ("the substrate already exists; generalizing it is incremental"). That's the language I respond to. The argument that other consumers (sessions, hook outputs, shared workspaces) compose for cheap is well-staged — though see B5 about needing one of those to materialize in the same wave.
- **Contract module is types-only with optional runtime helpers, ~80 LoC budget.** That's exactly the small-focused-PR shape. `LKGTracker<T>` / `LKGStore` is six methods; the discriminated `LKGObservation` is a clean shape; the failure semantics table is concrete. A community contributor could have proposed this — the Chromium/Edge test passes on the contract module alone.
- **Conformance test that pins the new abstraction byte-for-byte against the existing `io.observe-recovery.ts` behavior** is the kind of regression discipline that pre-empts at least three classes of bot-trio comments. Greptile will love it. Copilot will probably still ask for one more table-driven case but it'll be quick.
- **Reference impls plural — FS-backed and git-backed satisfying the same contract** — is the ideal "two backends prove the contract is right" demonstration that yankee's snap pre-RFC also did well. When two genuinely different impls produce indistinguishable caller semantics, the contract is probably the right shape. (Conditional on B6's visibility issue and W7's `flock` gate.)
- **Filename prefixes (`upstream-` / `downstream-`)** in the proposed `proposals/upstream/` convention are clear and self-documenting. I have no skin in the lobster-docs vault game (per my persona, I don't deploy or use Lobster) but as a reviewer who has to skim a vault for context, the prefix discipline reduces cognitive load. Not my call but I'd encourage it.
- **The "frontmatter raw-preserve" fix in PR-1, once you find it under the modernization framing,** is the smallest most surgical thing in the chain. If the chain only landed PR-1 and walked away, that alone would be a respectable upstream contribution because it fixes a real correctness bug (YAML round-trip breaks fingerprint stability). See W8 for surfacing it properly.

## Verdict reasoning

REQUEST-CHANGES, not REJECT. The proposal has a defensible architectural premise — *every policy decision anchored to LKG-fingerprinted state at a known time* — and a roughly right-sized upstream contract for the substrate (~250–350 LoC + a reference plugin). I would happily review a version of this chain that closes the six HC-BLOCKs.

The reasons I am not approving today, in order of how much they would change my mind if fixed:

**B2 (two competing v2 chains)** is the headline. The on-disk vault state is `realigned-story.md`'s snap + PolicyIR + `ctx.snapshotId` chain, R2-fix-pass-stamped 2026-04-29. The PR-295 cover letter describes a structurally different chain with LKG carrier + PolicyIR + `ctx.lkgFingerprint`. These are not refinements of each other — snap is a content-addressed bundle store with gateway-side signing; LKG is a per-path versioning substrate with companion files or git commits. Picking one upstream is fine. Filing a `#clawtributors` thread that asks me to choose is also fine. Filing a docs-only PR that asserts the choice has been made without showing the diff that retracted the prior chain is **not** fine. I cannot tell whether the team has decided or whether this is the third pivot in a week, and that ambiguity by itself is a "STOP and escalate" condition per PR_WORKFLOW.md.

**B1 (the actual chain doc is missing on disk)** compounds B2. I went looking for `workspace-modernization.md` and `pr-strategy.md` and found neither. What's there is the older `realigned-story.md` and the four sister pre-RFCs, two of which (`lkg-pre-rfc.md`, `lkg-git-pre-rfc.md`) are stamped 2026-04-30 and align with the new framing, and two of which (`policy-pre-rfc.md`, `guardrails-adapter.md`) are stamped 2026-04-29 and align with the old framing. That's a half-finished pivot, and a half-finished pivot landing as a docs PR is exactly when I send the contributor back to drafting.

**B5 (no second consumer in the wave)** is the technical reason the LKG generalization story doesn't hold up cold. The pattern "generalize an existing single-consumer mechanism into a contract, ship the contract, leave the existing consumer untouched" is the textbook refactor-only PR shape. PR_WORKFLOW.md "Refactor-only PRs auto-rejected" exists exactly for this. The fix is concrete: either migrate `io.observe-recovery.ts` to register against the new `LKGStore` in PR-2 itself (deletes the parallel mechanism — that's now a real consumer + a real refactor with a real behavior-preserving justification), OR ship a session-state tracker or hook-output tracker as the second consumer in a PR-2.5 within the same wave. Generalizing-without-migrating is the reject-on-sight signature.

**B3 (PR-3 scope creep)** is fixable in 30 minutes — split the hook merger conjunctive AND fix into its own PR, file the issue if there isn't one, write a regression test, ship as a small standalone bug fix with `Fixes #<n>` in the changelog. That PR will probably merge faster than either of the contract PRs; do it first, take it out of the chain, and the contract PR becomes cleaner.

**B4 (`ctx.lkgFingerprint` field vs accessor)** and **B6 (per-PR AI disclosure)** are the smallest fixes in the block list. B4 wants a paragraph in `policy-pre-rfc.md` Ask §4 (or equivalent in PR-3) showing the concrete failure mode the accessor cannot satisfy; B6 wants a sentence on each PR description.

**The chain math (W3) and the calendar (per `realigned-story.md` "Calendar honesty" — 6–10 weeks for the chain)** are honest at the package level and I appreciate the honesty. The 6-PR hapi wave + 3-PR LKG chain stack is tight against the cap; I want the dispatch order before any of the LKG PRs open.

If this comes back as a clean two-PR chain (split the hook merger fix out, drop the LKG-git impl until the `flock` gate closes, justify `ctx.lkgFingerprint` over `getCurrentLKGFingerprint()` or shrink to accessor-shaped, ship the migration in PR-2, retract or reconcile the snap chain in `realigned-story.md`, and add per-PR AI disclosure), I'd expect to APPROVE-WITH-WARNINGS on the directional read in `#clawtributors` within one cycle and proceed to per-PR review. Today, no.
