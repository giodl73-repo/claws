## Summary

Stand up the two foundation packages: `@bic/openclaw-workspace` (consolidates
the scattered `claws-sdk/src/workspace/` tree + new canonicalize helper + full
AST subpath shipping parse + emit + classify + 11 embedded PEG grammar strings

coherent. CP-11 handles policy + snap; CP-12 handles CLI + E2E.

means fewer coordination points, but each resulting package is larger (6-7
subpaths each). The atomic `@openclaw/*` → `@bic/openclaw-*` rename happens
during absorption.
