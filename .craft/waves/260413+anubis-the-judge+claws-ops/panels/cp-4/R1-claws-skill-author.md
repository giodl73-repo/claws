## Summary

The scaffolding pipeline (`claws new skill`) is clean and delivers on the "adopt in 5 minutes" promise for Tier 1 declared skills. The annotate and publish domains are well-structured with good separation between SDK and CLI. The key management domain is functionally complete but the ceremony UX needs polish for real-world use. The two highest-impact issues are: (1) the publish command never loads fleet policy from config, silently bypassing the governance gate that enterprise teams would rely on; and (2) the badge system reads from SKILL.md which does not exist in scaffolded skills, breaking the scaffold-annotate-publish golden path.
