## Retain through substantial rewrite

- direct questions that separate reassurance from observable action;
- reader routing by a current situation;
- evidence, counterevidence, unknowns, and context;
- manager-transition continuity;
- the shift from personal output to team conditions;
- fair credit, context, opportunity, and resource advocacy;
- reciprocal diligence before joining;
- explicit decision criteria and reversible next steps;
- caution when ambiguous changes may involve safety, retaliation, or a formal
  employment process;
- common traps as misuse warnings;
- concise checklists and public-module links.

## Remove

- employer names, levels, ladders, calibration, and internal process;
- named score patterns, gradients, urgency labels, and pattern classifiers;
- score sums, gates, resets, corrections, and arithmetic;
- promotion probability, timing, correlation, accuracy, and outcome forecasts;
- fixed 30-, 60-, 90-day, cycle, or multi-year clocks;
- deterministic stay, transfer, follow, return, or exit recommendations;
- candidate, manager, or employee inference;
- online profiling or former-report surveillance;
- manager rankings and advocacy concentration by promotion readiness;
- signal batteries, contamination narratives, escape windows, and paranoia
  reinforcement;
- legal, severance, reference, performance-plan, or investigation advice;
- generated studies presented as external evidence.

## Public guide architecture

The modules remain authoritative. Guides are question-led reading paths:

1. getting an honest read;
2. when support does not become action;
3. when a manager changes;
4. starting as a manager;
5. advocating fairly for a team;
6. choosing a role, manager, or environment;
7. deciding whether to stay, transfer, or leave;
8. when something feels wrong;
9. when access is uneven.

Each guide should:

- use only accepted public module semantics;
- introduce no new empirical outcome claim;
- provide one small practice;
- state what it cannot determine;
- include a safe stop or route condition;
- link to the relevant blank toolkit record;
- remain independently reviewable.

## High-risk transformations

| prospective-manager scoring | use reciprocal diligence without person classification |
| stay-or-move math | use explicit criteria, options, uncertainty, and reversible tests |
| managed-out signal battery | record direct facts, distinguish explanations, protect safety, and route serious concerns |
| remote score penalty | inspect access pathways without arithmetic or protected-data inference |
