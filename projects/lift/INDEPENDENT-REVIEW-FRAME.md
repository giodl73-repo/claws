# Independent Review Frame

This is private coordination context for the public LIFT review protocol.

## Thesis

The role-reviewed modules already work as public design drafts.

The missing shared capability is a bounded way for an independent person to
attempt one module without author coaching and report usability or safety
findings without disclosing a workplace record.

## Owner system

- modules own their definitions, practices, claims, examples, and learning;
- `.roles/` owns synthetic design assurance;
- `EVIDENCE_POLICY.md` owns claim labels;
- `ETHICAL_USE.md` owns permitted and prohibited use;
- `PUBLICATION_POLICY.md` owns sanitization.

The review protocol does not replace any of these.

## V1

- one public review protocol;
- synthetic scenarios;
- one privacy-safe report form;
- one GitHub issue form;
- one public index of admitted independent reviews;
- a clear distinction between attempt, usability evidence, golden status, and
  validation.

## Non-goals

- recruiting or identifying reviewers;
- collecting completed profiles;
- observing private conversations;
- comparing reviewers;
- measuring career outcomes;
- certifying the framework;
- marking any module independently reviewed before evidence exists;
- paying reviewers through this repository;
- creating research consent, legal, labor, or ethics-board machinery.

## Comparison

| Analogue | Disposition |
|---|---|
| module synthetic role review | reuse finding and disposition structure; do not call independent |
| usability task attempt | adapt one task, think-aloud friction, and stop outcome |
| public issue template | adapt for structured sanitized intake |
| testimonial or star rating | avoid |
| formal human-subjects research | avoid claiming; route separately if proposed |
| employment or manager evaluation | prohibit |

## Smallest evidence slice

- representative accepted input: Scenario A, one Module 00 dimension;
- structured failure: Scenario E, prohibited centralized scoring;
- observable proof: completed public report form or issue;
- deletion mapping: unsafe content is locked, redacted, or deleted;
- admission rule: no evidence-state change until a real independent attempt is
  accepted.

## Deletion target

Delete review theater:

- author-guided demonstrations described as independent;
- synthetic roles described as people;
- one review described as validation;
- testimonials without method;
- AI-generated reports described as independent-human review;
- reports containing private employer facts;
- unusable feedback that cannot be tied to an artifact and attempted task.

## Disproof

The protocol should be revised or retired if independent people cannot:

- understand the review boundary;
- complete a synthetic attempt without author coaching;
- report friction without exposing private data;
- identify what the attempt does and does not support;
- use the findings ledger without implying validation.
