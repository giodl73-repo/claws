# EC-026 and EC-034 Evidence Role Review

## Review Lenses

- Gateway/runtime owner.
- Lobster container and deployment owner.
- Platform/fleet operator.
- CISO/security.
- Founder/skeptical simplicity.

## Findings Applied

### EC-026

- Keep the first Rust proof side-effect free. Parsing and admission evidence
  cannot accidentally claim command execution or effect authority.
- Require device identity, protocol overlap, a declared command subset, and
  idempotency before the bounded invocation is accepted.
- Treat the Cargo executable as fork proof, not as a canary or ownership
  transfer.
- Keep TypeScript authoritative until a disposable Gateway connection,
  admission-time routing, restart, migration, release, and deletion proof
  exists.
- Do not require plugin or skill migration for the first node profile.

### EC-034

- Accept OpenClaw Fleet tenant cells as the second independent target family;
  they are deployment units rather than another Lobster version-slot variant.
- Do not mistake per-cell operation leases and rollback for portable
  multi-unit parent/child fan-out.
- Keep convergence a projection over unit-owner evidence.
- Keep purge, authority revocation, admission halt, process stop, and external
  effect settlement separate.
- Preserve optional single-host Fleet and ordinary local operation without a
  controller.

## Decision

Both contracts move from C to B. Each now has a dedicated owner/source
baseline and bounded proof. Neither is evidence-ready:

- EC-026 still lacks a real Gateway canary, lifecycle/state migration,
  operational budgets, release proof, and TypeScript deletion gate.
- EC-034 still lacks portable target-set acceptance, recovered fan-out,
  targeted repair, break-glass propagation, and containment-effect certainty.
