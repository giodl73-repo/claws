Admin and Governance

Collaboration and ownership opportunities across Scout, MAC/MOS3, Partner Center, and OpenClaw

Omar's vision: Own tenant/global settings, policy, permissions, approvals, audit/override state, Partner Center, marketplace governance, and M365 auth decisions.

Purpose

This brief frames a menu of areas where the Admin & Governance team could lead, co-own, or help close decisions. It is not a fixed assignment. Possible seams include native Scout availability, Partner Center, tenant/global authority, permissions and approvals, marketplace governance, M365 authentication, and the unified admin experience.

Product vision

Scout needs one enterprise control plane where administrators can configure availability and policy, govern extensions, understand effective state, approve or override safely, and audit consequential decisions.

  • MAC/MOS3 and Partner Center remain authoritative for Microsoft catalog, title availability, distribution, and native M365 admin decisions.
  • Lobster/Scout composes those decisions with enrollment, tenant configuration, policy, and runtime state and presents a coherent experience.
  • OpenClaw owns generic runtime configuration, policy, evidence, and conformance primitives.
Guardrail: Use one authoritative source for each security or availability decision. Missing identity, permission, or first resolution must not silently permit protected behavior; transient failures may retain an explicit previous-good decision but must never widen access.

Current work in flight

Lobster #5422 enforces native MAC/MOS3 availability for the existing Scout Teams app after operator enrollment. On the 2026-07-27 snapshot it was open and non-draft, with GitHubPop failing, the required Runtime PR context missing, and review not current. Live state must be rechecked.

The intended contract is already bounded:

  • Scout is an existing Teams tabs app, not a new declarative agent.
  • Do not invent a separate Lobster availability setting or policy surface.
  • MAC/MOS3 title availability and block state are authoritative.
  • Lobster validates the environment title ID and stable AppId, resolves with a user OBO token, caches per caller, and composes explicit denial with enrollment.
  • The armed gate fails closed before first success; later refresh failure retains the last-known decision rather than widening access.
  • Enforcement remains default-off until live validation succeeds.

Success measures

  • Native Scout availability is proven and safely enabled.
  • The Partner Center/M365 package is submitted or submission-ready with named external owners.
  • Tenant/global settings have durable authority, inheritance, API, preview, explain, audit, and rollback.
  • Marketplace outcomes are enforceable without turning Lobster into a competing registry.
  • M365 auth, incident response, telemetry, support, and long-term ownership are explicit.
